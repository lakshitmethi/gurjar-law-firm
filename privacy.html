Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 September 2026

1. Information We May Process

Depending on the features enabled, the portal may process account identifiers, user names, role information, case details, contact information, fee-related information, and other data entered by authorized users for case-management purposes.

2. Google Sign-In

If Google Sign-In is enabled, the portal may receive basic account information provided through Google's authentication service, such as an account identifier, name, and email address, as permitted by the sign-in configuration. Google authentication is used to verify eligible users and does not by itself determine portal permissions.

3. How Information Is Used

  • To authenticate users and control access to portal features.
  • To display and manage authorized case-management information.
  • To support administration, security, troubleshooting, and portal operation.
  • To provide information to users according to their assigned role and permissions.

4. Browser Storage and Hosting

The portal may use browser storage, such as local storage, to retain certain settings or data on a user's device. Where online hosting, authentication, database, or other third-party services are configured, those providers may process technical or account information as necessary to provide their services.

5. Data Sharing

Information should only be made available to authorized users or service providers as reasonably necessary to operate the portal, comply with law, protect security, or provide the requested service. The portal is not intended to sell user information.

6. Security

Reasonable access controls should be used to protect portal information. No internet-based system can be guaranteed to be completely secure, so users should protect their credentials and use the portal only on trusted devices and networks.

7. Data Accuracy and Retention

Authorized users are responsible for keeping information accurate where they are permitted to edit it. Information may be retained for as long as reasonably required for case management, legal obligations, security, or administration, subject to the firm's applicable practices and law.

8. Your Choices

Users may contact Gurjar Law Firm & Associates regarding access, correction, or other questions about information associated with their portal use, subject to applicable law and professional record-keeping requirements.

9. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when portal features, service providers, or legal requirements change. The current version will be published on this page.

10. Contact

For privacy questions, contact Gurjar Law Firm & Associates through the contact details provided by the firm.
